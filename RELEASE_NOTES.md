# PULSE 1.0.2

## Live Goal Tracking

Choose one saved goal in **Settings → Notifications → Live Goal Tracking**. PULSE shows your progress in an Android notification and updates it when health data syncs. Tap the notification to open the goal, or use **Stop tracking** to turn it off.

Goal details are hidden by default on the lock screen. You can also hide the notification entirely. Android 16 and later support an enhanced progress display.
